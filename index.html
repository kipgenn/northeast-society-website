A student-led society representing students from Northeast India at IIT Delhi. Established on 18 January 2026, the society seeks to create a home away from home for its members, celebrate the rich cultures and traditions of Northeast India, and foster meaningful friendships within the IIT Delhi community.

Farewell, Batch of 2026

0:00 / 0:00

Some years ago, the Batch of '26 arrived at IIT Delhi as complete strangers. Somewhere between the fest season chaos, tea breaks and jamming sessions, we became a family.

Set to a cover of 'Vaisohlhu' produced by our own NESIITD students, a song about looking back on the good old days, this is our small goodbye to the graduating class. Thank you for everything. You will always have a home at NESIITD.
