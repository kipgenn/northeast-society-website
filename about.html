Our Heritage

The Northeast Society acts as a home away from home for students at IIT Delhi. We bridge the gap between the Eight Sister States and campus life.

Beyond being a student group, we are a practical support system and a community. Whether it's organizing our annual Northeast Experience (NEO) or meeting for community dinners, we help students stay connected to their roots.

Celebrating Our Diversity

The Northeast is a diverse region made up of many different cultures, languages, and histories. At Northeast Society, we don't just group everyone under one label. We recognize and respect the specific regional and community identities that make our members who they are.
