Our Story

IIT Delhi can get pretty overwhelming. NESIITD is a home away from home. We set it up so you'd always have a place to fall back on.

We're not a formal club, we're your batchmates and seniors. Grabbing dinner when you're homesick, random jammings, tea breaks, we make sure you don't have to do college alone.

Northeast is a collection of completely different tribes, languages, and histories. NESIITD respects those exact differences. No matter which state or community you're from, you have a voice here.
