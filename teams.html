The Committee and Faculty Advisors of Northeast Society

‎

It brings me immense joy to witness the birth of the Northeast Society, IIT Delhi. What began as a simple idea has now become a reality, one that is a blessing and will continue to serve and benefit many people in the years to come. This achievement would not have been possible without the dedication, commitment, and sacrifices of our team. Their hard work and belief in this vision transformed an aspiration into a proper society. For that, they deserve the deepest appreciation and recognition.

As we move forward, I hope that this society continues to be a home away from home for its members, a place where friendships are formed, cultures are celebrated, and future generations of students find support, belonging, and inspiration.

— C. Lalrinpuia, President
